Estimated range

The range calculation always assumes a complete battery depletion cycle, meaning driving the entire distance the battery is empty in one go. Commuting journeys with repeated cold starts and recurring battery heating activation are not included. The displayed range indication is based on the WLTP partial profiles, taking into account additional parameter inputs for outside temperature, air conditioning, payload, and wheel/tire combination. The actual range may vary in practice, as crucial factors such as personal driving style, speed, use of comfort and auxiliary systems, topography, and the aging and wear process of the battery are individually variable. Due to its significantly higher efficiency compared to a combustion engine, the electric motor contributes less to overall energy consumption, meaning the electric vehicle is considerably more sensitive to, for example, auxiliary systems, as well as aerodynamic and tire influences.
